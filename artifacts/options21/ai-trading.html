The opportunity

A faster way to investigate what you see on a chart

You may recognise a potentially useful pattern but struggle to define exactly what is happening.

Perhaps two short-term timeframes need to agree. Perhaps the setup only appears to work when the broader trend is aligned. Perhaps you want to know whether an indicator adds useful information or merely makes the chart look convincing.

Claude can help you convert those observations into clearer research questions.

The purpose is not to produce an instant trading signal. It is to help you investigate your own ideas more systematically.

Research, not prediction

Use the workflow to ask better questions about your charts, define conditions more clearly and keep a repeatable record of what the evidence does—and does not—support.

Common questions

Questions traders usually ask

Do I need to know how to code?+
No. Claude can assist with drafting and explaining Pine Script. You must still check that the code accurately represents your intended rules.
Will Claude tell me what to trade?+
The setup is designed for research, not recommendations. Claude can help investigate chart conditions, but it should not make your trading decisions.
Can it analyse several timeframes?+
Yes, subject to the capabilities of the connection and your TradingView configuration. The session includes a multi-timeframe research template.
Does the session include building my trading system?+
No. We use one defined example to teach you the workflow. Developing or validating a complete proprietary trading system is a separate project.
Can it backtest my idea?+
Claude can assist with defining rules and developing Pine Script for TradingView’s testing tools. Whether a particular idea can be reliably tested depends on how precisely its rules can be defined and the historical data available.
Can it place trades for me?+
No. Live execution and broker integration are outside the scope of this service.
Are the tools guaranteed to keep working?+
No. Third-party software, interfaces and subscription terms can change. The setup depends on continuing compatibility and permitted use.
