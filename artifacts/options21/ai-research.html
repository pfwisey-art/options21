Why it matters

The two checks that make AI research safe to use

Build-time
The Research Protocol

A standing set of rules your workspace follows while it builds a report: pin one share price and use it everywhere, calculate multiples rather than copy them, never present an estimate as a fact, reconcile the price target to the valuation, and never fabricate a figure, source or chart. It forces the report to show its working.

After the fact
The Adversarial Accuracy Audit

A separate, independent pass — run fresh so it can't rubber-stamp its own work — that re-extracts every number, recomputes it from source, and produces a discrepancy register: what's inconsistent, what the correct figure is, and whether it changes the conclusion.

Together they turn a confident-looking draft into something you have actually verified. That is the difference between using AI and trusting it — and it's the part almost no course teaches.
