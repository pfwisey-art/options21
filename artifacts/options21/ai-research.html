Why it's different

The verification method that sets this setup apart

The Research Protocol (applied as each draft is built)
Transparent working, from the first line

A documented standard your workspace applies while it builds a draft: one canonical price used throughout, multiples calculated rather than copied, estimates clearly distinguished from facts, the price target reconciled to the valuation, every figure traceable to a source. Your drafts show their working.

Separate verification pass (before you rely on it)
An independent second look at the numbers

A second-pass adversarial check re-examines sources, calculations, valuation assumptions and internal consistency, and produces a verification record and an exception list identifying anything requiring human resolution.

This is an automated quality-control process — not an external audit, assurance engagement or guarantee of accuracy. An internally consistent report can still rest on a wrong price or an outdated filing, so human review remains essential.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need coding experience?+
None. If you can use a browser, you can run this. The session covers everything end to end.
What do I need before the session?+
A computer, your own Claude account (or willingness to set one up), and permission to install on that machine. I'll send a short prerequisites list when you book; if anything blocks full setup on the day, we finish it during your support period.
Is my data processed locally?+
No. Although the software is accessed from your computer, Claude processes requests in the cloud through your own account. You should not enter confidential or price-sensitive information you are not authorised to disclose.
Do I need my own subscription?+
Yes — you pay Claude, the plugin and any data sources directly, additional to the session fee. I'll show you exactly what to get.
What do the protocol and verification pass do?+
The protocol builds each draft to show its working and trace its sources; the separate pass re-checks material figures and flags discrepancies for you to resolve. They reduce error risk — they do not guarantee every error is caught, and they are not an external audit.
Will this give me stock tips?+
No. This is education and technical training. It helps you prepare and check your own research so you can make your own informed decisions. It is not personal financial advice, and the first-report review does not endorse any security, thesis or price target.
Do I own it afterwards?+
The configured workflow is yours to use, subject to the continuing availability and terms of the third-party software and subscriptions it depends on.
I'm not in Australia — can I book?+
Yes, by video call. Coverage depends on the reliable data sources available for your market.
